2. Mark any page

Open the page you want to annotate and click the bookmark.

  • Vertical / Horizontal: click to place a full-screen guide, or hold and drag it into position before releasing.
  • Circle / Arrow: click and drag. Hold Shift for a perfect circle or snapped arrow.
  • Select and move: grab any existing outline or guide and drag it. Click a mark to select it, then change its color or width, or press Delete. Select mode lets you click empty space without drawing.
  • Quick colors: red, green, and blue buttons apply to the selected mark and future marks. Click empty space in Select mode to deselect first.
  • Grid: toggle an alignment grid with 32 px spacing by default. Choose 8, 16, 24, 32, 64, or 128 px. The horizontal margin (↔) sets the left/right inset, and the vertical margin (↕) sets the top/bottom inset. Both default to 32 px. Margin controls step by 8 px; hold Shift for 1 px adjustments. Cmd + Up/Down jumps between 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px; values between presets jump to the next preset in that direction. While the grid is on, changing a margin shows its current width and height in pixels. These measurements fade out after you pause. Grid lines start at those margins. The grid stays visible when you hide the toolbar, and does not snap or move your marks.
  • Move the toolbar: drag the SAGA MERK header to reposition the frosted-glass toolbar. It stays inside the viewport. You can also focus the header and use arrow keys (10 px steps; Shift for 1 px).
  • Text: choose Text and click to place a box. Type a multiline note. Text has no background: drag anywhere on a box to move it, and double-click to edit. While editing, drag over the text to select characters. Click outside to finish editing. Escape asks for confirmation before closing Saga Merk and discarding annotations. Cancel keeps your content; press H outside a text field to hide the toolbar instead. Selected text uses the color controls. Undo restores text edits and moves. Hide toolbar also hides text selection outlines.
  • Tool shortcuts: 1 Select, 2 Vertical, 3 Horizontal, 4 Circle, 5 Arrow, 6 Text, 7 Browse. Shortcuts are inactive while typing in fields. Choosing a tool by number also reveals a hidden toolbar.
  • Browse: interact with the page while marks remain visible.
  • Undo / Clear: undo drawing, movement, color changes, or deletion—or clear everything. Clear can also be undone.
  • Hide toolbar: hide the controls and selection highlight for a screenshot. Hiding switches automatically to Browse mode so you can use the page while preserving your marks. Press H outside a text field to bring the toolbar back; it restores the tool you were using before hiding.
  • Escape: confirm before closing and discarding all marks. The prompt reminds you that H hides the toolbar without losing content. The close button and clicking the bookmark again also ask for confirmation.

Choose a color and line width, annotate, then take your usual screenshot and share it with your AI.

Marks stay fixed to the viewport, so frame the page before drawing. Refreshing or closing clears them. Runs locally with no downloads or network requests. Browser settings pages, PDF viewers, and some sites with strict security policies may block bookmarklets.
