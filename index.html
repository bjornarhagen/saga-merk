Precise when it matters.

Use a 32 px grid, adjustable margins, and full-page guides to spot alignment issues. Hold Shift for perfect circles, squares and snapped arrows. Hold Option/Alt to draw shapes from the center or place mirrored guides; combine it with Shift.

Change your mind freely.

Drag marks to move them. Select page elements to reveal Move, Edit text and Delete controls plus editable CSS in the toolbar. Left/right arrows navigate nearby elements; up/down change their layer within the current stacking context; an invisible placeholder keeps the surrounding layout in place. Double-click text to edit it. Click or drag with Counter to place 1, 2, 3 and onward. Shift adds a colored circle with a white number; Option/Alt adds sub-counters such as 1.1 and 1.2. Text size adjusts notes and counters, starting at 24 px with options from 8 to 96 px. Select an element and choose Edit text to change its text, color, and size. Choose a color, undo a change, or clear the page and start fresh.

Grid settings, shortcuts & the finer details

Grid spacing: 8, 16, 24, 32, 64 or 128 px. Left/right and top/bottom margins default to 16 px. Arrow keys change margins by 8 px; Shift changes them by 1 px. Option/Alt + Up/Down jumps through 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024. Live width and height measurements fade after a pause. The grid is a visual reference; it doesn’t snap your marks.

The toolbar opens in the bottom-right corner. Drag its header to move it. H hides it and enables Browse mode; H again restores your previous tool. Escape asks before closing and discarding your annotations. Delete removes a selected mark; Cmd/Ctrl + Z undoes changes outside Browse mode. Shortcuts leave text fields alone. Option/Alt-drag a page element to move it directly, or Shift-click to edit text. Select a page element and choose Edit text; color and text size apply to the focused text. Text-only wrappers are handled automatically; larger containers start with their first text block. Click Done to finish. Edits stay local and remain visible when you hide the toolbar. Undo reverts edits; Clear or closing Saga Merk restores the original page, including moved or deleted elements.
